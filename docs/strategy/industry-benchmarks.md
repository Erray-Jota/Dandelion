## Financial Metrics — Industry Benchmarks

### Average Order Value (AOV)

| Segment | Industry benchmark | Alameda-adjusted target for Dandelion |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Everyday retail (independent) | $65–$90 | **$85–$115** |
| Everyday retail (upper independent) | $78–$110 | Stretch target with add-ons + premium mix |
| Grocery / mass floral | $65–$85 | Not your competitive set |
| Wedding-led shops | $185–$340 (per event order avg.) | Track wedding contract value separately |
| Holiday peak (Valentine's, Mother's Day) | Often 20–40% above baseline | Seasonal planning metric |

**Dandelion current signal:** Catalog runs $65–$310; homepage shows strong mid-tier ($65–$140) with premium options ($180–$310). Target blended everyday AOV of **$95+** is realistic given market income.

**How to improve AOV:**
- Card add-on ($6.50) on every order → +$1,500–$3,000/year at modest attach rates
- Premium anchors on shop page (show $180–$310 next to $65–$95)
- Delivery minimum for distant ZIPs
- "Deluxe" vs. "Classic" variants on top 5 SKUs

### Gross Margin

| Metric | Healthy benchmark | Warning zone | Notes |
|--------|------------------|--------------|-------|
| **Gross margin on fresh arrangements** | **65–72%** | Below 60% | SAF / Paul Goodman CPA benchmark |
| Many independents (actual) | 45–58% | — | Underpricing + waste are main causes |
| Net profit margin (after all overhead) | 8–15% | Below 5% | Well-run shops at high end |
| Wholesale spend as % of revenue | 28–35% | Above 40% | COGS discipline indicator |
| COGS as % of retail price (per arrangement) | 20–33% max | Above 33% | Per SAF pricing guidance |

**Margin by channel (blended, independent shops):**

| Channel | Typical gross margin |
|---------|---------------------|
| Walk-in / phone everyday | 40–50% |
| Wedding / event | 50–60% |
| Corporate weekly contracts | 45–55% |
| Wire-service incoming (net of commission) | 35–45% |
| Direct web delivery | 45–65% (depends on delivery cost allocation) |

**Dandelion target:** **62–68%** blended gross margin on everyday arrangements; **55–60%** on weddings after labor/install.
