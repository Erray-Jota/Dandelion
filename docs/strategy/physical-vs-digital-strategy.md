# Physical vs. Digital Strategy Map

**Purpose:** Clarify which revenue strategies require in-person effort, outbound sales, or operational labor — vs. what can be built and scaled through digital marketing and website systems.

**Last updated:** July 25, 2026

---

## Quick Reference

| Effort type | Best for | Examples |
|-------------|----------|----------|
| **Digital-first** | Scale without proportional labor | SEO, checkout upsells, email reminders, Google Ads |
| **Physical / outbound** | High-LTV B2B relationships | Corporate accounts, funeral homes, venue partnerships |
| **Hybrid** | High-value services | Weddings, memorials, subscriptions |
| **In-shop / delivery** | AOV lift at point of contact | Designer upsell, driver handoff, pickup experience |

## Strategy-by-Strategy Breakdown

### 1. Raise Average Order Value (AOV)

| Tactic | Type | Effort | Notes |
|--------|------|--------|-------|
| Card add-on at checkout ($6.50) | **Digital** | Low | Squarespace checkout or post-add-to-cart prompt |
| Premium price anchoring on shop page | **Digital** | Low | Merchandising — show $180–$310 next to $65–$95 |
| "Deluxe" vs. "Classic" product variants | **Digital** | Low–Medium | Set up in Commerce; one-time config |
| Vase upgrade options | **Hybrid** | Medium | Digital selection; physical fulfillment |
| Delivery minimum by zone | **Digital** | Low | Shipping/local delivery rules in Squarespace |
| Designer upsell at pickup/walk-in | **Physical** | Ongoing | Train staff on one upsell question |
| Phone order upsell script | **Physical** | Ongoing | "Would you like to add our handwritten card for $6.50?" |

**Verdict:** Mostly **digital** — highest ROI for least labor. In-shop/phone upsell is a small physical add-on.

## Decision Matrix: When to Go Physical vs. Digital

```
                    HIGH LTV per account
                           │
     Corporate accounts ●  │  ● Weddings
     Funeral homes      ●  │  ● Events
                           │
  PHYSICAL ◄───────────────┼───────────────► DIGITAL
  OUTBOUND                 │                MARKETING
                           │
     Farmers markets   ●  │  ● SEO / local search
     Chamber events     ●  │  ● Checkout upsells
                           │  ● Email / SMS reminders
                           │
                    LOW LTV per account
```

**Rule of thumb:**
- **Digital** when: transaction is self-serve, repeatable, and doesn't require trust-building at scale (everyday gifts, add-ons, SEO, reminders)
- **Physical** when: contract value is $500+, relationship determines repeat referrals, or product is emotionally high-stakes (weddings, sympathy)
- **Hybrid** when: digital generates the lead, human closes the deal (weddings, corporate)

| Activity | Suggested weekly time |
|----------|----------------------|
