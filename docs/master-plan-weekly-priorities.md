# Dandelion Master Plan — Weekly Priorities for Maximum Revenue Impact

**Single consolidated plan:** Website development · Revenue strategy · AI agents · Outbound sales  
**Last updated:** July 26, 2026  
**Principle:** Every week focuses on **2–3 high-impact moves** — not everything at once.

---

## How This Plan Is Prioritized

Work is ranked by **expected revenue impact per hour invested**, not by task category.

| Priority tier | Definition | Examples |
|---------------|------------|----------|
| **P0 — Conversion leaks** | Visitors or buyers are lost today | Homepage popup, duplicate products, broken checkout flow |
| **P1 — AOV & margin** | More revenue/profit per existing order | Card upsell, premium anchors, margin recost |
| **P2 — Retention & LTV** | Same customers buy again | Post-delivery email, occasion capture, subscriptions |
| **P3 — Acquisition** | New customers find you | SEO titles, Google Business, landing pages |
| **P4 — High-LTV channels** | Weddings, corporate, funeral homes | Forms, outbound, relationships |
| **P5 — Scale & automate** | Agents and systems reduce labor | Reminder agent, analyst agent, churn predictor |

**Weekly time budget (recommended):** 4–8 hours/week owner + optional 2–4 hours staff  
**Rule:** Finish P0 before P3. Never skip data upload (Week 1) — it validates everything else.
