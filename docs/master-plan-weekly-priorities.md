1. **POS median AOV:** $49 → **$75**
2. **Shop gross margin %:** 31.5% → **38%**

## How This Plan Is Prioritized

Work is ranked by **expected revenue impact per hour invested**, informed by measured baselines — not by task category.

| Priority tier | Definition | Examples |
|---------------|------------|----------|
| **P0 — Conversion leaks** | Visitors or buyers are lost today | Homepage popup, duplicate products, broken checkout flow |
| **P1 — AOV & margin** | More revenue/profit per existing order | POS hero ladder, card upsell, stem-buy discipline, recost |
| **P2 — Retention & LTV** | Same customers buy again | Square email capture, win-back, occasion capture, subscriptions |
| **P3 — Acquisition** | New customers find you | SEO titles, Google Business, landing pages |
| **P4 — High-LTV channels** | Weddings, corporate, funeral homes | Forms, outbound, relationships |
| **P5 — Scale & automate** | Agents and systems reduce labor | Reminder agent, analyst agent, churn predictor |

**Weekly time budget (recommended):** 4–8 hours/week owner + optional 2–4 hours staff  
**Rule:** Finish P1 (POS AOV + margin) before P4 (weddings/corporate). Online traffic is not the bottleneck.
