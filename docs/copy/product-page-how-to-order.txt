HOW TO ORDER

Step 1 — Check your delivery area
We deliver to Alameda, Oakland, and Berkeley. Pickup is available at our shop: 1548 Webster St, Alameda.

Step 2 — Choose your delivery date
Delivery: Tuesday–Friday, 9 am–4 pm · Saturday, 9 am–12 pm
Saturday orders must be placed by Friday at shop close (5 pm).
We do not deliver Sundays or Mondays.

Step 3 — Add to cart, then select Delivery or Pickup
After entering your address at checkout, choose Delivery or Pickup. Add a gift message at checkout — we'll handwrite your card.
